Privacy Policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

What we collect

  • Information you give us on our intake form and by email: your name, email, phone, city and state, resume, job preferences, work authorization answers and any links you share.
  • Payment details are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Emails you send us, and messages employers send about your applications if you forward them to us.

How we use it

  • To find matching jobs and submit applications on your behalf, which means sharing your resume and application answers with the employers you apply to.
  • To send you updates about your applications, run your mentorship and interview prep, and manage your account and billing.

What we never do

  • We never sell or rent your personal information.
  • We never share your resume with anyone except the employers you apply to and the service providers who help us run Job Pulse (such as email, payment and form tools), and only as needed to provide the service.
  • We never put your personal information in public posts. The jobs we share on social media never include client information.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your plan is active and for up to 12 months after it ends, so you can restart easily, unless you ask us to delete it sooner. Records we must keep for tax or legal reasons are kept as long as the law requires.

Your choices

Email us any time to get a copy of your information, correct it, or have it deleted. We'll respond within 30 days. You can opt out of non-essential emails by replying "unsubscribe".

Security

We use reputable providers and limit access to your information to the people and tools that need it to provide the service. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't send us passwords, Social Security numbers or bank details. We will never ask for them.

Contact

Message the Job Pulse team. See also our Terms of Service.
