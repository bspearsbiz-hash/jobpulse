VIP Payment Agreement

Last updated November 26, 2026

By paying, I agree to the full VIP Package price of $4,000: $1,000 today and $250 per month for 12 months, charged automatically to my card. This is a 12-month payment plan. I can't cancel future payments at will, but I can pause for up to 2 months, and if I get no interview invitations in my first 90 days, Job Pulse cancels all my remaining payments. Missed payments pause service until paid.

Payment schedule

  • $1,000 down payment, charged when you sign up.
  • $250 a month for 12 months. The first $250 is charged one month after you sign up, then once a month on that same date.
  • Total: $4,000. The plan ends automatically after the 12th monthly payment. Nothing renews.
  • Payments are processed by Stripe to the card you use at checkout. You can update your card any time by emailing us.

Services

For 12 months from your sign-up date, the VIP Package includes:

  • 30 job applications every weekday, sent for you on real company job boards, with priority on fresh postings.
  • A private 1-on-1 interview prep session with the Job Pulse team every week, plus a mock interview before each real interview you book.
  • A resume rewrite and LinkedIn profile rewrite, done within your first two weeks.
  • Tailored cover letters for your best-fit roles.
  • Help weighing job offers and negotiating salary.
  • Group interview prep calls every Tuesday and Thursday, 5–6 PM Mountain Standard Time (Arizona).
  • Priority support from the Job Pulse team by email, answered first, the same business day.
  • Your private dashboard showing every application, its link, and anything that needs you.

Missed or failed payments

If a payment fails, we'll let you know by email. Service pauses until the payment goes through and picks back up once it does.

Pausing your plan

You can pause your VIP Package for up to 2 months in total over the plan, for any reason (a new job's trial period, travel, a family matter). Ask by email or on the contact page at least 3 days before your next payment date. While paused, your monthly payments and your applications both stop, and your plan end date and remaining payments move back by the same amount of time. Pauses are taken in whole months, as one pause or two.

90-day interview guarantee

If you get no interview invitations in your first 90 days of applications, you can end the plan and we cancel all of your remaining monthly payments. To use it, email us within 14 days after day 90. It applies when:

  • You sent us your completed intake and resume, and approved (or asked for changes to) your rewritten resume within 7 days of receiving it.
  • You told us about every employer reply through your dashboard or by email, and answered the questions we asked you so we could keep applying.
  • Your applications weren't paused during those 90 days for a missed payment or at your request (paused time doesn't count toward the 90 days).

Payments already made are not refunded under this guarantee. It cancels what's left. Phone screens and recruiter screening calls count as interview invitations.

Other changes

Apart from a pause or the interview guarantee, you can't cancel or change future payments yourself. Any other change, including ending the plan early, only happens by written agreement (email is fine) between you and Job Pulse.

No guarantee of a job

Apart from the 90-day interview guarantee above, we guarantee the work described above, not the outcome. Interviews and offers are up to employers. Everything else in our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy also applies.

Questions

Email us any time before or after you sign up.
