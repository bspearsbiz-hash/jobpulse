Payment schedule

  • $1,000 down payment, charged when you sign up.
  • $500 a month for 6 months. The first $500 is charged one month after you sign up, then once a month on that same date.

  • Payments are processed by Stripe to the card you use at checkout. You can update your card any time by emailing us.

Services

For 6 months from your sign-up date, the VIP Package includes:

  • 30 job applications every weekday, sent for you on real company job boards, with priority on fresh postings.

  • A resume rewrite and LinkedIn profile rewrite, done within your first two weeks.
  • Tailored cover letters for your best-fit roles.
  • Help weighing job offers and negotiating salary.

  • Priority 24/7 support from the Job Pulse team.
  • Your private dashboard showing every application, its link, and anything that needs you.

Missed or failed payments

If a payment fails, we'll let you know by email. Service pauses until the payment goes through and picks back up once it does.

Changes

You can't cancel or change future payments yourself. Any change to the plan, including pausing or ending it early, only happens by written agreement (email is fine) between you and Job Pulse.

No guarantee of a job

We guarantee the work described above, not the outcome. Interviews and offers are up to employers. Everything else in our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy also applies.
