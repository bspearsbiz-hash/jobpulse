1. What Job Pulse is

Job Pulse is a job search assistance service. On a monthly plan, we find job postings that match the preferences you give us and submit applications on your behalf, and we offer mentorship, interview prep and group sessions while your plan is active.

Job Pulse is not an employer, a staffing firm or a placement agency. We do not have special access to employers, we do not get paid by employers, and we do not charge any fee based on whether you are hired or what you are paid.

2. No guarantee of interviews or a job

Hiring decisions are made only by employers. We do not promise or guarantee interviews, job offers, a particular salary or a hiring timeline. What your plan includes is the work: the number of applications per weekday shown on your plan, plus the support described on our site.

3. Your authorization

By completing our intake form, you authorize Job Pulse to search for jobs and submit applications in your name, using the resume and information you provide, to roles that match the preferences you gave us. You can change your preferences or ask us to stop applying at any time by emailing us.

4. Accurate information

You confirm that the resume and answers you give us are true and accurate. We only use the information you provide. We never invent experience, education, credentials or other facts on your behalf. If an application asks something we can't answer truthfully from what you gave us, or asks you to sign an agreement or certification, we skip it or ask you first.

5. Plans, billing and cancellation

  • Plans are billed monthly through Stripe and renew automatically each month until you cancel: $99/month (10 applications per weekday) or $199/month (20 applications per weekday). Members who joined before November 25, 2026 keep the price they signed up at.
  • The VIP Package is a 6-month payment plan: $1,000 at sign-up and $500 a month for 6 months ($4,000 total). It is not cancellable by the member and is covered by the VIP Payment Agreement, which controls if it differs from these Terms.
  • Monthly plans: you can cancel at any time by emailing us. Cancellation stops future charges. Applications, mentorship, interview prep and support stop when your plan ends.
  • Applications start the next weekday after we receive your completed intake and resume. Weekends and US federal holidays are not counted.
  • If we fall short of your plan's daily application count, we make up the difference on following weekdays.
  • Questions about a charge? Email us and we'll review it with you.

6. Communication

Employers will contact you directly at the email and phone number on your applications. Please tell us about interview invitations so we can help you prepare. We may email you about your applications and your account.

7. Your responsibilities

Please respond to employers yourself, attend your own interviews and tell us if any information changes. Do not ask us to apply anywhere you are not legally allowed to work or to provide false information.

Our total liability for any claim is limited to the amount you paid us in the month before the claim.
