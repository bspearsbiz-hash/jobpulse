Questions, answered

How does Job Pulse work?

You tell us the jobs you want. Our team finds fresh openings that match and submits real applications for you every weekday, right on the employer's own job board. You get a private dashboard with every application and its link.

Why does applying early matter?

Most roles get the bulk of their applicants in the first few days, and recruiters often start screening the first batch. We focus on fresh postings so you’re near the top of the pile, not buried at applicant 500.

Is this mass-applying?

No. We only apply to roles that match the titles, pay and location you set, on the employer’s own hiring page, so your applications reach the recruiter instead of getting filtered out as spam.

When do applications start?

The same day we get your intake and resume.

What kinds of jobs do you apply to?

Any field: healthcare, trades, office, tech, sales and more. Remote, hybrid or in person in your city, in the US and Canada.

Are these real applications?

Yes. Every one is submitted on the company's own hiring page, and you can open each link on your dashboard to see it.

Do you help with interviews?

Yes. Live group interview prep with the Job Pulse team every Tuesday and Thursday, and 1-on-1 prep on our top plan.

Do you guarantee a job?

No one honestly can. We guarantee the work: real applications every weekday and real prep when interviews come.

Is my information safe?

Your resume and answers are only used to apply for you, and your dashboard is private to you. We never sell your information.

Can I cancel?

Monthly plans, anytime. VIP is a 6-month plan (see the VIP terms).
