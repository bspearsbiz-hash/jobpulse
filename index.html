Questions

When do applications start?

The next weekday after we get your intake form and resume.

What kind of jobs do you apply to?

The roles you tell us you want, at real companies. Fully remote by default, and hybrid or in-person in your city if you say so on your intake.

Do you guarantee a job?

No one honestly can. What we guarantee is the work: your full plan count of real applications every weekday, plus prep so you're ready when interviews come.

How do I know what you applied to?

You get regular progress reports with every company, role and link, plus a heads-up whenever something needs you.

How long do mentorship and support last?

As long as your monthly plan is active. If you cancel or your plan stops, applications, mentorship and support stop too.

Can I cancel?

Anytime. Just email us and we'll take care of it.
